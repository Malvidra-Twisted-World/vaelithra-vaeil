# Part IV: The Infinite Archive

This chapter begins after the characters resolve their encounter with Sōyō Gumi in Part III. Ra accompanies them to the Infinite Archive, where their account of the journey helps Vaeil understand the silver-eyed woman's place in history. The chapter ends with the transfer of the Archive, the scattering of Vaeil's soul, and the characters' return to Elysgard.

The woman is already one of Vaeil's Twelve Fragments. Her origin lies at the end of this chapter, when a fragment travels a few months into the past of the same timeline. See "The Closed Loop" in *Running the Adventure*. The characters can discover this connection before the scattering confirms it.

## The Sun's Witness

When Ra arrives, read the following text, adjusting the description if the characters have left the bridge:

>>Golden light spills across the mountain road. The knights lower their heads, and the champions who arrived ahead of them kneel. A traveler in a white robe walks between them, gold thread stirring at his cuffs. He stops beside the wounded before looking toward the woman traveling with you.
>>
>>"You have come a long way," he says. "May I walk with you?"
>>

Ra is the Sun God, though he introduces himself simply as Ra. Kiyomasa and the Sun Rays acknowledge him without being commanded. He asks for anyone restrained during the encounter to be released and hears the characters' account of the dispute. Any agreement made with Kiyomasa remains in effect unless the characters wish to renegotiate it; Ra's arrival does not remove injuries or reverse deaths.

***Roleplaying Ra.*** Ra is warm, direct, and protective. His attention returns to practical matters whenever conversation becomes abstract: whether someone can walk, whether a wound needs tending, and whether the woman wants the protection being offered. He respects Vaeil but is comfortable questioning him. He knows neither the woman's identity nor the reason the Archive has begun to change.

***Ra's Promise.*** Years ago, Vaeil asked Ra to guard the road to the Infinite Archive and welcome anyone whom history could not explain. Ra agreed without knowing whom he would meet. He can share the following information:

- The woman is being sought because of reports of an unexplained presence on the eastern front. Ra has not ordered her imprisonment.
- The Infinite Archive preserves the lives and memories of Dewanegara. Its entrance is concealed from travelers who have not been granted passage.
- Vaeil entrusted Ra with access to the hidden road. Reaching the Temple of Vaeil at Elysgard does not give an intruder access to the Archive.

***Development.*** Ra offers to take the characters and the woman to Vaeil. Kiyomasa and the Sun Rays remain on the mountain road. If the characters want time to treat the wounded or settle an outstanding promise, Ra waits.

## The Hidden Path

Once the characters agree to accompany Ra, read:

>>Ra steps to the edge of the mountain and raises a hand. Sunlight gathers beneath his palm. A white stair appears beyond the cliff, followed by another, descending through open sky. Far below stands a solitary door in a silver frame. Upon it is the emblem of a book bound without a spine.
>>

The stair is solid beneath the characters' feet. Beyond its edges, the sky has no visible horizon. Ra has opened a route to the Archive rather than an ordinary passage down the mountainside. He walks beside the characters while the woman moves ahead, drawn toward the door.

***The Door.*** The door has no handle or keyhole. When the woman approaches it, the emblem glows and the door opens. Ra can admit the expedition by Vaeil's permission; the door's response to the woman is unexpected. She recognizes the emblem without remembering where she has seen it.

***Development.*** The entrance remains open while the expedition enters. Ra accompanies the characters inside. The path can be opened again for their return; entering the Archive does not trap them there.

## The Infinite Archive

Read the following text when the characters enter:

>>Endless shelves rise beneath a sky without stars. Pale streams of light run between them, and books drift from shelf to shelf. Beneath an ancient silver-leaved tree, a man sits at a wooden desk, writing. He finishes a sentence before looking up.
>>
>>His gaze rests on each of you, then on the woman beside you. He sets down his quill and draws several chairs toward the desk.
>>
>>"Welcome home," he says. "Tell me what happened."
>>

Vaeil is the man at the desk. He greets Ra as an old friend and recognizes the characters from their audience at Elysgard. The woman is unfamiliar to him, although her presence resonates with his own. He has not yet identified that resonance as a fragment of his soul.

### Archive Features

The following features apply throughout the Archive:

***Light.*** The streams between the shelves shed bright silver light. No sun or other source is visible overhead.

***Books.*** The shelves hold accounts of lives, events, and choices. A book brought down for the characters can be read in a language each reader understands. The Archive preserves differing recollections as differing recollections; writing a false statement does not change the event it describes.

***Finding an Account.*** Vaeil can retrieve a record identified by a name, place, or event. During this chapter, accounts concerning the expedition also drift toward the desk as those events are discussed. No check is required to find a record the characters can identify from their journey.

***Time.*** The Archive stands outside ordinary time, but the expedition's visit proceeds normally for the characters. They do not age backward, regain expended resources, or return to an earlier point in the adventure by moving among its shelves.

### Vaeil's Desk

The desk holds silver parchment, an unfinished record, and the quill Vaeil was using when the characters arrived. The woman sits close enough to see the writing but does not touch it without permission.

***Roleplaying Vaeil.*** Vaeil is patient, quietly humorous, and exhausted by the war. He asks about individuals before armies. He answers questions honestly and admits uncertainty without making a mystery of it. His interest in the woman never takes precedence over her comfort or the characters' account.

***What Vaeil Knows.*** Vaeil can provide the following information during conversation:

- The thirteenth toll was unprecedented. It prompted him to send the characters east to investigate the anomaly.
- His older agreement with Ra concerned anyone history could not explain. It was a precaution, not a prediction of the woman's arrival.
- The Archive contains no record of the woman's birth. Her presence belongs to history, but its point of origin has not yet been recorded.
- He has searched for a way to end the Sundering War without erasing either people. He has not found a way to secure such a future.

***The Expedition's Account.*** Vaeil asks the characters to describe the journey. Their journal, recovered objects, and recollections all serve as testimony. He is particularly interested in the people they saved or lost, the woman's conduct at Hollow Refuge, and the treatment they received from the rival factions. He accepts separate accounts where witnesses disagree.

As individuals are named, books containing their histories gather around the desk. Their accounts reflect the events of Parts II and III, including deaths and broken promises. The woman adds a small detail about someone the characters remember, then admits that she remembers nothing before the war.

***Development.*** When the characters have given their account, one book remains open before the woman. It contains the first record of her arrival in Dewanegara. Continue with "The First Page."

### The First Page

When the characters examine the open book, read:

>>The page shows a ruined field beneath a smoke-darkened sky. A woman with silver eyes stands alone among broken weapons. Beside the image is a date only a few months before your expedition. A thin silver line runs from the page, across the desk, and into the unwritten parchment beneath Vaeil's hand.
>>

The image records the woman's earliest memory. Earlier pages contain no account of her birth or childhood. The silver line connects her arrival to an event that has yet to occur. This connection is visible without a check. A character who succeeds on a DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) check determines that the line runs backward from the unwritten event to the dated page. On a failed check, its direction remains unclear.

Vaeil studies the same evidence. If the characters suggest time travel, he agrees that it would explain the missing beginning, but he cannot yet identify the future event. The woman recognizes the battlefield and can describe her first days there; she does not remember choosing a destination or traveling through time.

***The Archive Awakens.*** As she touches the page, books lift from shelves throughout the Archive. Streams of light approach her hands and pause. She chooses to reach toward them. When the light touches her, she experiences the memories preserved within the books, including the lives described by the characters.

The woman becomes aware of the Archive as a living whole. She can sense its shelves and distinguish the stories calling from them. This awakening does not create her soul; it allows an existing fragment to assume the Archive's care.

***Naming Vaelithra.*** Vaeil offers her the name **Vaelithra**. She repeats it and accepts. The books settle around her, and the silver streams return to their channels. Questions and conversation can continue during this change; the woman remains herself, with the same memories of the expedition.

## The Book Beyond History

After Vaelithra accepts her name, an untitled book appears beneath the silver-leaved tree. Its pages are blank until Vaeil and Vaelithra place their hands upon it. The book then reveals possible futures. Ra and the characters can read it alongside them.

>>Silver lines spread across the parchment. You see a street rebuilt after the war, then the same street in ruins, then a forest where it once stood. A familiar face appears among the crowd. The page opens outward until the street seems close enough to enter.
>>

### Histories That Might Be

The characters can step into the scenes presented by the book. Their bodies remain beside the tree while their perceptions move within a possible future. They can speak with its inhabitants, examine its surroundings, and return by withdrawing from the page. These scenes do not change the past or restore the dead. Objects found within them cannot be removed, but the characters can record what they learn.

Use the following possibilities, drawing on people and places established during the expedition. Each reveals its central cost through conversation or examination without requiring an ability check.

***Divine Victory.*** A home or refuge the characters know has been restored. Its Infernal inhabitants are absent, and the remaining residents cannot remember them. Household records omit their names. An object associated with one of the missing people remains, but nobody recognizes its owner. This future ends the war by erasing the Infernal people.

***Infernal Conquest.*** A survivor aided by the characters has obtained revenge. The armies that destroyed their home are defeated, but the fields have failed and medicine no longer reaches the settlement. The survivor can describe what victory cost and what they still need. The mortal world is collapsing beneath the war's aftermath.

***The Unchanging Peace.*** A child is playing beside an undamaged home. The same game begins again whenever it ends. Nearby, a healer changes a bandage that is always bloodied beneath the fresh linen. Conversation reveals that the child has not grown and the wound has never healed. The war was prevented by holding the world in stasis.

***Recording the Visions.*** Anything the characters write, draw, or dictate about these futures appears in the book's margins beside their names. Vaeil considers these accounts as he searches for a future worth protecting. He does not require the characters to agree with him, and they can question his rejection of a possibility.

### The Final Possibility

When the characters have examined the visions, the book reveals a future in which the Gods prevail and the Infernal people survive. The war ends without ending hatred, but people can rebuild and change. Vaeil chooses to pursue this possibility.

The page also shows an ordinary scene reflecting the expedition's consequences: someone the characters saved rebuilding a home, a former enemy sharing a table with refugees, or a survivor carrying on the work of someone who died. Details recorded in the margins appear in the scene. A death that occurred during the adventure remains part of this future.

***The Cost.*** As Vaeil studies the page, silver writing appears beneath the vision:

>>**Probability: Near Zero.**
>>
>>**History cannot reach this future alone.**
>>
>>**Intervention Required.**
>>
>>**One Keeper is insufficient.**
>>

A second image shows Divine and Infernal hands reaching toward the Archive. Its new possibilities will draw people seeking protection, control, or the destruction of what they fear. Vaeil realizes that protecting the future requires guardians distributed across history. He can entrust the Archive to Vaelithra and send fragments of his own soul into other ages.

***Speaking with Vaeil.*** Before acting, Vaeil explains his intention and asks what the characters would ask of those carrying his soul. They can offer requests, propose alternatives, object to his decision, or say goodbye. He considers their words but accepts responsibility for the sacrifice himself. No check determines whether he is permitted to make that choice. Vaelithra records any requests as counsel for the fragments, rather than commands binding them.

## The Scattering of the Soul

Vaeil transfers his authority over the Infinite Archive to Vaelithra. The silver streams answer her, and she can now retrieve and preserve its records without his help. He asks her to accompany the fragments when they awaken across history and guide those who lose their way. She promises to do so.

When Vaeil removes his pendant and begins the scattering, read:

>>Silver light escapes between Vaeil's fingers. Where it passes, his hand becomes transparent. More light gathers at his shoulder, his chest, the edges of his face. Each thread separates and hangs above the open book. Beneath them, its pages fill with dates scattered across ages you have never known.
>>

Vaeil divides his soul into twelve fragments. Vaelithra is one of those twelve, already present because her fragment travels into the past. Transferring the Archive's authority did not create an additional soul.

### The Returning Fragment

One light settles above the dated image of the battlefield from "The First Page." Read:

>>Within the light, the ruined field appears again. The date is unchanged: a few months before your journey. The silver-eyed woman draws her first breath among the broken weapons.
>>
>>Beside you, Vaelithra touches her chest. The light pulses with the same rhythm.
>>

The departing fragment is the woman the characters brought to the Archive. They can compare the date, question her, and follow the silver line back to the event now taking place. If they identify the loop, Vaeil confirms their conclusion. Otherwise, his own recognition makes the connection clear:

>>Vaeil looks from the page to Vaelithra, then to you.
>>
>>"It reached you," he says. "Then this part of the plan has already worked. You helped her find the way back."
>>

The light enters those earlier months. Vaelithra remains beside the characters with all the experiences she has already lived. There is one timeline: her arrival helped cause the decision that sent her there. The adventure does not restart, and the characters' actions remain part of its history.

### The Waiting Fragment

The other fragments depart for their destinations until only one light remains. The book displays the words *Awaiting Bearer*. This fragment has no destination yet.

Ra offers his Solar Crest as a place for it to wait. Once the fragment settles inside, he closes the crest and promises to deliver it when its bearer can be found. All twelve fragments have now left Vaeil. His remaining silhouette is the last trace of his presence, rather than another fragment.

***Vaeil's Farewell.*** Vaeil thanks Ra, Vaelithra, and the characters. His final words are quiet: "Every page deserves to be turned." His silhouette fades, his smile disappearing last.

***The Thirteenth Bell.*** The Bell of Time rings thirteen times. Its echoes cross the Archive and the ages beyond it. Ra rests one hand on the Solar Crest and the other on Vaelithra's shoulder. The age of the Twelve Fragments has begun.

## Concluding the Adventure

Vaelithra preserves the characters' account of Vaeil's final day. If their journal survives, she copies its pages and returns the original. If it was lost or never used, she provides a fresh book in which they can write, draw, or dictate their testimony.

***The Public Account.*** Vaelithra asks what the characters intend to tell Silverlight. They can report the sacrifice openly, keep the route and the Archive's new abilities secret, or leave separate accounts where they disagree. She retains the full record within the Archive. Decisions about what to disclose do not alter that record.

***Treasure.*** Vaelithra gives the party the *Keeper's Quill*, described in *Appendix A: Magic Items*. It preserves the memory of Vaeil's final day and contains no soul fragment.

### Return to Elysgard

Ra opens the return route to Elysgard. Kaede accompanies the characters if she remained with the expedition. The sanctuary has continued to receive refugees during their absence, and its inhabitants want news of those still beyond the eastern front.

The characters' reception depends on their journey. Survivors of Hollow Refuge give their own accounts of the rescue. Names of those who died are added to the Hall of Memory. If Kaelion or Mordren was spared or aided, a messenger may bring thanks, questions, or another demand concerning the Catalyst. If either commander died, their followers remember the characters' part in it.

Eiren asks what happened to Vaeil and what help Silverlight can expect. An open account allows the order to prepare for life without its founder. If the characters conceal the sacrifice, Eiren must continue with incomplete information; she asks them directly what they know rather than accepting an unexplained assurance.

The adventure ends after the characters have had an opportunity to fulfill an outstanding promise, visit a survivor, or add a name to the Hall of Memory. A sentence from their journal can close the session: the first witnesses' account, preserved in the Archive alongside the history they helped bring about.

# Vaelithra VI: Vaeil

## *When History Was Given a Name*

> *"History is not written by those who win. It is written by those who choose to remember."*
>
> — **Vaeil, Keeper of the Archive**

---

## Adventure Summary

The **Sundering War** has reached its darkest days.

Once sparked by a dispute over recognition between the Divine and Infernal races, the conflict has grown into a devastating war that engulfs the whole of **Dewanegara**. Hatred spreads faster than truth, and every passing day claims countless innocent lives.

Amidst the chaos stands **Silverlight**, the first order devoted to **Vaeil**, the God of Time and Keeper of the Archive. Rejecting political allegiance, Silverlight dedicates itself to preserving history, rescuing civilians, and ensuring no life is forgotten, regardless of race.

When an unexplained temporal resonance is detected near the eastern front, a group of Silverlight operatives is dispatched to investigate.

Instead of discovering a weapon...

they find a nameless woman with silver eyes.

As rumours begin calling her **The Catalyst**, armies from both sides race to capture her.

Unbeknownst to anyone, this single encounter will become the beginning of the greatest secret ever recorded within the Archive.

---

## Adventure Background

Long before the modern age, the continent was known simply as **Dewanegara**.

For centuries, the Divine and Infernal races coexisted beneath an uneasy peace. Yet many among the Infernal people believed the very name *Dewanegara* ("Realm of the Gods") symbolised their exclusion from a world they also called home.

Their appeals for recognition were repeatedly ignored.

Some Divine leaders dismissed the complaints as insignificant.

Others believed acknowledging the Infernal race would weaken Divine authority.

Resentment slowly became unrest.

Unrest became violence.

Violence became war.

The conflict would later be remembered as **The Sundering War**.

Not every god desired conquest.

Not every demon sought revenge.

Countless soldiers fought only because they had no choice.

Among those refusing to take sides stood **Vaeil**, God of Time and Keeper of the Archive. Rather than participating in the war, Vaeil founded **Silverlight**, an order devoted to preserving history, protecting civilians, and safeguarding the knowledge that would survive long after kingdoms had fallen.

As the war intensified, Vaeil sensed an impossible distortion within the flow of time.

A resonance that should never have existed.

Unable to leave the Archive himself, he entrusted a small Silverlight expedition with a simple task.

> **Investigate the anomaly.**

---

## Adventure Hooks

All player characters are members of **Silverlight's First Generation**.

Regardless of their origins, every member has abandoned political allegiance in favour of preserving life and history.

Characters may include:

- Former Divine soldiers who refused to participate in the massacre of civilians.
- Infernal refugees who now rescue victims regardless of race.
- Battlefield physicians.
- Archivists responsible for preserving history.
- Scholars researching temporal anomalies.
- Guardians assigned to protect Silverlight expeditions.

Every member of Silverlight lives by the **Three Oaths**:

> **Record the Truth.**
>
> **Protect the Living.**
>
> **Guide Those Who Have Lost Their Way.**

---

## Running the Adventure

Unlike most adventures, **Vaelithra VI: Vaeil** places little emphasis on defeating a central villain.

Instead, the story focuses on uncovering the origins of Human Vaelithra, witnessing the truth behind the Sundering War, and revealing why Vaeil ultimately chose to create the Twelve Fragments.

Combat encounters exist as consequences of war rather than battles against evil.

Whenever possible, encourage diplomacy, compassion, and moral judgement over unnecessary violence.

---

## Character Level

**Recommended Level:** 7

**Party Size:** 4–5 Characters

Level 7 characters possess enough experience to survive the chaos of the Sundering War while remaining insignificant compared to the gods themselves.

This adventure is not about changing history through strength.

It is about witnessing the moment history itself changed forever.

## Dungeon Master's Notes

This adventure serves as the chronological beginning of the **Vaelithra Saga**, taking place during the final years of the **Sundering War**, before Vaeil divided his soul into the Twelve Fragments.

Although the players witness many pivotal historical events, they are **not meant to alter history**. Instead, they become witnesses to the very moment that shaped the future of Dewanegara.

The central conflict of this adventure is **not good versus evil**.

Both the Divine and Infernal races contain individuals driven by hatred, compassion, ambition, sacrifice, fear, and hope.

The players should be encouraged to see the war through multiple perspectives before reaching the Archive, where the truth behind the conflict and the origin of Human Vaelithra are finally revealed.

# Part I: Silverlight Sanctuary

> *"History is not protected by those who raise their swords.*
>
> *It is protected by those who remember why the swords were drawn."*
>
> — Inscription above the Hall of Memory

## Overview

This chapter introduces the party to **Silverlight**, the first religious order devoted to **Vaeil**, the God of Time and Keeper of the Archive.

Unlike the kingdoms of Dewanegara, Silverlight has refused to participate in the Sundering War. Instead, its members dedicate themselves to preserving history, rescuing civilians, and protecting knowledge from being lost to war.

The chapter establishes the core themes of the adventure before sending the party to investigate a mysterious temporal anomaly unlike anything ever recorded.

## Silverlight Sanctuary

Silverlight serves as both sanctuary and archive.

Every member follows the teachings of **Vaeil**, believing that history belongs to every living soul regardless of race or allegiance.

Within its walls can be found:

- A medical hall treating wounded soldiers from every faction.
- The Hall of Memory, recording the names of every victim of the war.
- The Grand Library containing rescued books from ruined cities.
- Living quarters shared by members from countless races.
- A central chapel dedicated to Vaeil.

No weapons are drawn inside the sanctuary.

No banners of war are permitted.

Here...

everyone is simply another life worth saving.

---

## The Three Oaths

Every member of Silverlight lives according to three sacred oaths.

> **Record the Truth.**
>
> **Protect the Living.**
>
> **Guide Those Who Have Lost Their Way.**

---

## Allow the Players to Explore

Give the party several minutes to introduce their characters and explore the sanctuary.

Possible roleplaying opportunities include:

- Helping physicians treat wounded civilians.
- Recording stories from refugees.
- Returning damaged books to the Grand Library.
- Speaking with Divine and Infernal survivors.
- Visiting the Hall of Memory.

During these conversations, emphasize one recurring idea:

> Silverlight does not ask,
>
> "Which side are you on?"
>
> It asks,
>
> "Who still needs saving?"

### Hall of Memory

Thousands of silver plaques cover the walls.

Each bears a single name.

No rank.

No race.

No nation.

Only a life that once existed.

Members of Silverlight silently place fresh flowers beneath newly engraved names each morning.

### The Bell of Time

Without warning...

A deep bell echoes across the sanctuary.

**DONG**

Everyone pauses.

Another follows.

**DONG**

Then another.

Again.

Again.

Again.

The bell continues ringing.

Three.

Five.

Seven.

Nine.

Twelve.

The sanctuary grows silent.

Some members quietly begin praying.

Others look toward the chapel in confusion.

Then...

For the first time in recorded history...

The bell rings once more.

**DONG**

The thirteenth bell.

Silence.

No one speaks.

A young archivist drops the scroll he had been carrying.

A battlefield physician freezes in place.

Even the children stop playing.

An elderly High Archivist whispers with trembling lips.

> "Impossible..."

Another quietly replies.

> "The Bell of Time has no thirteenth toll..."

---

### DM Information

The Bell of Time has long served as Silverlight's warning system.

Its known meanings are preserved within Silverlight tradition.

| Toll | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Lord Vaeil summons Silverlight. |
| 3 | A great tragedy has occurred. |
| 5 | An Archive Relic has been discovered. |
| 7 | A distortion has appeared within history. |
| 9 | A god has fallen. |
| 12 | Every High Archivist must gather immediately. |
| **13** | Unknown. No historical record exists. |

The players are not expected to understand its meaning.

Neither does Silverlight.

The players never physically see Vaeil.

His voice resonates naturally throughout the chamber.

No divine pressure fills the room.

No overwhelming power.

Only warmth.

Only calm.

Even in the middle of history's greatest war...

# Part II: Hollow Battlefield

> *"War does not choose who is worthy of death.*
>
> *Only who is unfortunate enough to stand where it arrives."*

---

# Overview

Leaving the safety of Silverlight Sanctuary, the party journeys toward the eastern front where Vaeil sensed an impossible anomaly.

The deeper they travel into the battlefield, the more they discover evidence of a mysterious figure who has been saving lives regardless of race or allegiance.

Their search ultimately leads them to a small refugee settlement known as Hollow Refuge, where they encounter the woman with silver eyes for the first time.

Before questions can be answered, two military patrols arrive simultaneously.

Each believes they are pursuing the same target.

"The Catalyst."

The village becomes trapped between two armies.

---

> "A young woman..."
>

>

# Hollow Refuge

Eventually the trail leads to a ruined farming village.

Though abandoned by both armies, dozens of civilians have secretly taken shelter inside.

Divine.

Infernal.

Human.

Children play together beneath collapsed rooftops while elderly refugees quietly prepare meals from dwindling supplies.

For the first time since leaving Silverlight...

The party sees hope.

---

# The Woman With Silver Eyes

Near the centre of the village, a young woman kneels beside a broken wagon.

She calmly repairs a damaged wheel while two children watch with fascination.

She wears no armour.

Carries no banner.

Bears no holy symbol.

Only a worn travelling cloak.

When spoken to, she is polite but distant.

She cannot answer even the simplest question.

"What is your name?"

...

"I don't know."

"Where are you going?"

"...Someone is waiting for me."

"Who?"

"I wish I knew."

---

# Development

Allow the players several minutes to interact with the woman and the villagers.

She instinctively helps anyone in need.

Without hesitation she:

- repairs equipment,
- treats wounds,
- comforts frightened children,
- shares food before eating herself.

Nothing about her appears extraordinary...

Until the alarms sound.

---

# Read Aloud

>
> "They're coming!"

Moments later...

A horn echoes from the western road.

Then another...

From the east.

Two patrols.

Both searching for the same person.

# Battlefield Events

At Initiative Count 20 (losing ties), resolve one Battlefield Event.

Roll 1d4.

| d4 | Battlefield Event |
|----|-------------------|
|1|A nearby house catches fire. Any civilian inside must be rescued before the end of next round.|
|2|A wounded refugee collapses in the open. An adjacent creature can use an action to drag them to safety.|
|3|Panicked children flee across the battlefield. They provoke opportunity attacks unless protected.|
|4|A burning cart collapses, creating difficult terrain in a 20-foot area.|

These events reinforce that the battlefield itself is as dangerous as the soldiers.

---

# Running the Enemy

Neither patrol initially targets the party.

Instead:

- Divine soldiers engage Infernal soldiers.
- Infernal soldiers return fire.
- Any soldier who spots the silver-eyed woman immediately changes priorities.

If either captain reaches her, they shout:

> "Stand down!"

> "You are coming with us!"

Neither intends to kill her.

Both believe capturing her will save countless lives.

---

# Running the Woman

Throughout the encounter, the woman refuses to kill.

On her turn she prioritises:

- carrying wounded civilians,
- stabilising the dying,
- shielding children,
- shoving attackers away,
- extinguishing fires.

Only if a civilian would otherwise die does she attack.

When she finally strikes...

Describe it.

---

# Read Aloud

Without warning...

She steps forward.

One soldier lunges.

She catches the spear with one hand.

Twists.

The weapon spins harmlessly through the air.

Before anyone can react...

The attacker is lying unconscious on the ground.

She never drew a weapon.

She simply continues helping the wounded.

---

# Ending the Encounter

As the fighting dies down...

One wounded Divine soldier looks toward her.

His eyes widen.

His voice barely escapes his lips.

> "...Silver eyes..."

He reaches for the sketch tucked inside his armour.

The portrait matches perfectly.

His face turns pale.

> "...The Catalyst..."

The woman freezes.

For the first time...

Fear crosses her expression.

Not fear for herself.

Fear for everyone around her.

She quietly bows her head.

> "I'm sorry..."

> "Everywhere I go..."

> "...people get hurt."

Without waiting for an answer...

She turns and begins walking toward the eastern mountains alone.

The anomaly Vaeil sensed...

Has just continued its journey.

# Part III: The Sun's Passage

> *"Faith is not always measured by what one believes.*
>
> *Sometimes it is measured by what one is willing to sacrifice."*

---

## Overview

After the battle at Hollow Refuge, the party continues east alongside the mysterious silver-eyed woman.

The path ahead leaves the war-torn plains behind, climbing into the mountains where the influence of the Sun God grows stronger.

Here, they discover that the woman is no longer being hunted by scattered patrols.

An organized order now searches for her.

Not out of hatred...

But because they believe capturing the **Catalyst** is the only way to save Dewanegara.

---

## Along the Mountain Pass

The road narrows into steep cliffs overlooking the eastern battlefield.

Smoke still rises in the distance, but the sounds of battle become faint echoes carried by the wind.

For the first time in days...

The party has a chance to breathe.

As the party exits the outpost, they find the mountain bridge ahead already occupied.

Several knights clad in white-and-gold armour stand in disciplined formation.

Unlike the soldiers encountered before, these warriors remain perfectly calm.

Weapons remain lowered.

One figure steps forward.

## Roleplaying Grand Captain Kiyomasa

Kiyomasa is not cruel.

Nor arrogant.

He sincerely believes his mission will prevent a greater tragedy.

He never insults Silverlight.

In fact, he openly respects them.

If questioned, he explains:

> "Our Lord teaches us that the sun gives life to all."
>
> "If the Catalyst truly exists..."
>
> "...then allowing her to wander freely may doom the very lives we seek to protect."

He refuses to elaborate further.

---

## Negotiation

The party may attempt to persuade Kiyomasa to stand aside.

Successful roleplay and ability checks can reveal:

- The Radiant Order has been receiving visions concerning the Catalyst.
- Even they do not know who she truly is.
- They have never been ordered to kill her.

If negotiations fail...

Combat begins.

# Part IV: The Infinite Archive

> *"Some doors are not opened by keys.*
>
> *They are opened by history itself."*

---

## Overview

The battle upon the mountain pass ends not in victory...

Nor defeat.

As the warmth of an impossible sunrise washes over the battlefield, every warrior instinctively lowers their weapon.

No command is given.

No miracle is performed.

Yet even the winds seem reluctant to disturb the moment.

Someone has arrived.

---

## The Sun's Witness

### Read Aloud

> Golden light spills across the stone bridge.
>
> It does not blind your eyes.
>
> Nor does it scorch your skin.
>
> Instead, it carries a quiet warmth unlike anything you have ever known.
>
> One by one, every knight of the Radiant Order kneels.
>
> Their helmets bow toward the eastern horizon.
>
> No voice commands them.
>
> They simply know.

A lone traveler walks through the light.

He wears no armor.

Carries no weapon.

Only a white robe trimmed with threads of gold that sway gently with each step.

The Grand Captain lowers his head.

> "My Lord..."

The traveler nods once, inviting him to rise.

---

## Meeting Ra

Ra greets neither friend nor foe.

His gaze moves calmly across the battlefield, pausing briefly upon the wounded from both sides.

Only after ensuring the conflict has truly ended does his attention settle upon the silver-eyed woman.

He studies her quietly.

There is no shock.

No recognition.

Only curiosity.

The same curiosity reflected upon everyone else's face.

---

### Roleplaying Ra

Ra speaks gently and with remarkable patience.

He never raises his voice.

He never assumes he understands more than those before him.

He simply observes.

If asked who the woman is, Ra answers truthfully.

> "..."
>
> "I do not know."

If asked why the Radiant Order sought her, he looks toward the Grand Captain.

> "Because I asked them to."

A pause.

> "Not because I knew who she was."
>
> "Because someone I trust asked me to prepare for this day."

---

## The Promise

If the party presses further, Ra finally explains.

Many years ago, Vaeil came to him with an unusual request.

Not a prophecy.

Not a warning.

Simply a promise.

### Read Aloud

> "He asked only one thing of me."
>
> Ra turns toward the rising sun.
>

Across the Infinite Archive.

Across every age.

Until...

The thirteenth chime resounds.

Silence follows.

Ra looks toward the empty place where Vaeil once stood.

"...So this was your answer."

He places one hand upon the Solar Crest.

The other upon the shoulder of the Infinite Archive's new Keeper.

No more words are needed.

Together...

They turn toward the endless shelves.

Behind them...

History quietly begins writing its next page.

*Thus began the age of the Twelve Fragments.*

> [!loot] The Keeper's Quill
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement by a creature proficient in Calligrapher's Supplies, or a Spellcaster)*
>
> A pristine silver quill once wielded by **Vaeil, the Keeper of the Infinite Archive**, before he entrusted history to Vaelithra and scattered his soul across time. The quill never dulls, never breaks, and never runs dry. When held, faint silver ink flows from its tip, though no ink reservoir can be found.
>
> This Vestige does not contain a fragment of Vaeil's soul. Instead, it preserves the memory of his final moments as the sole Keeper of History, ensuring that the beginning of the Archive's new age can never be forgotten.
>
> ___
>
> ### Archivist's Memory
> While attuned to this quill, you gain proficiency in the History skill. If you are already proficient, your proficiency bonus is doubled for History checks made to recall historical events, ancient civilizations, legendary figures, or magical artifacts.
>
> Additionally, you gain proficiency with Calligrapher's Supplies if you do not already have it.
>
> ___
>
> ### Record the Past
> Once per long rest, you may touch the quill to a blank sheet of paper and speak the command word.
>
> For the next minute, silver ink automatically records an echo of events that occurred within 30 feet of your current location during the last 24 hours.
>
> The record is entirely truthful but limited to what history itself witnessed. It cannot reveal thoughts, emotions, hidden intentions, or events prevented by magic that erased or altered history.
>
> ___
>
> ### The Unwritten Line
> Once per long rest, when you or a creature you can see within 60 feet fails an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you may use your reaction to invoke the quill.
>
> The failed roll is rerolled, and the new result must be used.
>
> As silver ink briefly appears in the air, the original outcome fades as though it had never been written.
>
> ___
>
> ### Keeper's Chronicle
> Whenever you spend at least 10 minutes writing with this quill, the writing can never fade due to age, water, fire, or mundane means.
>
> Magical attempts to alter or erase the writing automatically fail unless performed by a deity or an artifact of comparable power.
>
> ___
>
> ### Vestige of History
> The Keeper's Quill preserves the memory of **The First Page**, **The Last Possibility**, **The Promise of History**, and **The Scattering of the Soul**.
>
> Should every Vestige of History be gathered within the Infinite Archive, the memories contained within each Vestige may be experienced once more, allowing future generations to witness the true history of Vaeil's final day.

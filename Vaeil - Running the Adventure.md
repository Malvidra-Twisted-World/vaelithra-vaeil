# Running the Adventure

## *When History Was Given a Name*

> *"History is not written by those who win. It is written by those who choose to remember."*
>
> — **Vaeil, Keeper of the Archive**

This is for the Dungeon Master. It contains a complete adventure — *Vaelithra VI: Vaeil* — the chronological beginning of the Vaelithra Saga, taking place during the final years of the **Sundering War**, before **Vaeil** divided his soul into the Twelve Fragments.

> ##### Adventure Information
>
> **Recommended Level:** 7
>
> **Party Size:** 4–5 characters
>
> **Play Time:** 4–6 hours
>
> **Timeline:** Late Sundering War (B.S.)
>
> **Setting:** Dewanegara
>
> **Adventure Type:** Narrative • Investigation • War • Lore
>
> **Canon:** Vaelithra Saga VI

## Overview

Unlike most adventures, *Vaelithra VI: Vaeil* places little emphasis on defeating a central villain. Instead, the story follows the party as they uncover the origins of Human Vaelithra, witness the truth behind the Sundering War, and learn why **Vaeil** ultimately chose to create the Twelve Fragments.

Combat encounters exist as consequences of war rather than battles against evil. Whenever possible, encourage diplomacy, compassion, and moral judgement over unnecessary violence. Level 7 characters possess enough experience to survive the chaos of the Sundering War while remaining insignificant compared to the gods themselves — this adventure is not about changing history through strength, but about witnessing the moment history itself changed forever.

Here's an overview of what you'll find:

- **Running the Adventure.** This file: an overview of the war, the order of Silverlight, and guidance on running the adventure's themes.
- **Adventure Sites.** Four parts describe the party's journey from Silverlight Sanctuary, across the eastern battlefield, through the mountain pass, and finally into the Infinite Archive itself.
- **Appendices.** Three appendices describe the magic item, the homebrewed creatures, and an optional roleplay mechanic that appear in the adventure.

## Adventure Background

Long before the modern age, the continent was known simply as **Dewanegara**. For centuries, the Divine and Infernal races coexisted beneath an uneasy peace. Yet many among the Infernal people believed the very name *Dewanegara* ("Realm of the Gods") symbolised their exclusion from a world they also called home. Their appeals for recognition were repeatedly ignored. Some Divine leaders dismissed the complaints as insignificant; others believed acknowledging the Infernal race would weaken Divine authority. Resentment slowly became unrest. Unrest became violence. Violence became war. The conflict would later be remembered as **the Sundering War**.

Not every god desired conquest. Not every demon sought revenge. Countless soldiers fought only because they had no choice. Among those refusing to take sides stood **Vaeil**, God of Time and Keeper of the Archive. Rather than participating in the war, Vaeil founded **Silverlight**, an order devoted to preserving history, protecting civilians, and safeguarding the knowledge that would survive long after kingdoms had fallen.

As the war intensified, Vaeil sensed an impossible distortion within the flow of time — a resonance that should never have existed. Unable to leave the Archive himself, he entrusted a small Silverlight expedition with a simple task: *investigate the anomaly.* That expedition is the party.

Once sparked by a dispute over recognition between the Divine and Infernal races, the conflict has grown into a devastating war that engulfs the whole of Dewanegara. Hatred spreads faster than truth, and every passing day claims countless innocent lives. When an unexplained temporal resonance is detected near the eastern front, Silverlight dispatches the party to investigate. Instead of discovering a weapon, they find a nameless woman with silver eyes. As rumours begin calling her **the Catalyst**, armies from both sides race to capture her — and, unbeknownst to anyone, this single encounter becomes the beginning of the greatest secret ever recorded within the Archive.

Silverlight's home base is **Elysgard**, a hamlet that predates the order itself. Silverlight adopted it as one of its principal sanctuaries because a **Temple of Vaeil** already stood there. Vaeil himself visits occasionally, walking among the refugees with a book under his arm — to the people of Elysgard, this is unremarkable enough that no one kneels. Few outside Silverlight know that Elysgard's temple is also a secret point of access to the **Infinite Archive**; the path there is known only to Vaeil, those he personally trusts, and entities he has explicitly granted passage. The temple and the Archive are not the same place, and the players should never be able to reach one by finding the other.

### Adventure Outline

*Vaelithra VI: Vaeil* has four parts.

*Part I*, "Silverlight Sanctuary," introduces the party to Silverlight and Elysgard, the Sundering War, and the Three Oaths its members live by. An unprecedented thirteenth toll of the Bell of Time leads **Vaeil** to summon the party and send them to investigate a temporal anomaly beyond the eastern battlefield.

*Part II*, "Hollow Battlefield," follows the party across the war-torn front as they track a mysterious figure who has been saving lives regardless of allegiance. The trail leads to the refugee settlement of Hollow Refuge, where they meet the silver-eyed woman for the first time — moments before two rival hunting parties, the **Radiant Inquisition** and the **Ashen Covenant**, arrive to claim her as "the Catalyst."

*Part III*, "The Sun's Passage," takes the party into the mountains, where **Sōyō Gumi**, Ra's own Divine Host — and ultimately **Ra**, the Sun God himself — intercept them on the road to the Infinite Archive.

*Part IV*, "The Infinite Archive," reveals the truth behind Human Vaelithra, **Vaeil**'s final decision, and the birth of the Twelve Fragments.

### Adventure Themes

- History over Victory
- Neutrality in Times of War
- The Burden of Memory
- Hope Amidst Despair
- Sin and Virtue
- Identity
- Compassion Beyond Race

*Sin and Virtue* has an optional mechanical hook: see *Appendix C: Sins & Virtues Resonance* for a homebrew system that lets each PC's choices resonate with one of seven virtue-vice pairs — Pride/Humility, Envy/Kindness, Wrath/Patience, Greed/Charity, Sloth/Diligence, Gluttony/Temperance, and Lust/Chastity — with small roleplay and mechanical effects that shift as the party's choices shift, regardless of alignment.

## Silverlight and the Three Oaths

All player characters are members of **Silverlight's First Generation**. Regardless of their origins, every member has abandoned political allegiance in favour of preserving life and history. Characters may include former Divine soldiers who refused to participate in the massacre of civilians, Infernal refugees who now rescue victims regardless of race, battlefield physicians, archivists responsible for preserving history, scholars researching temporal anomalies, or guardians assigned to protect Silverlight expeditions.

Every member of Silverlight lives by the Three Oaths:

> **Record the Truth.**
>
> **Protect the Living.**
>
> **Guide Those Who Have Lost Their Way.**

Silverlight does not ask "Which side are you on?" It asks "Who still needs saving?"

### Order of Silverlight

Silverlight is not Vaeil's army — it is an order that follows his teachings, and Vaeil is not its commander. Its First Generation is led by six recurring NPCs the party can meet at Elysgard, none of whom fully agree with each other about the war:

| NPC | Role | View on the War |
|---|---|---|
| **Eiren Valora** (Human, Grandmaster) | Leads Silverlight because Vaeil trusts her, not because she is its strongest fighter. | "We stand with the living." Silverlight's neutrality must hold, even as it grows harder to maintain. |
| **Miyora Aster** (Human, Temple Keeper) | Tends the Temple of Vaeil at Elysgard; one of the few who treats Vaeil as an ordinary person. | Believes peace can still be found — and isn't afraid to tell Vaeil he's overthinking things. |
| **Hakon Veyr** (Half-Elf, Field Commander) | Leads Silverlight Knights into the field to evacuate civilians and stop massacres, killing only when necessary. | Believes the wounded must sometimes be protected with force, not just bandages. |
| **Sena Thorne** (Tiefling, Archivist) | Records every name of the war's victims and refugees. Never writes "casualty" — always a name, or *Unknown Child*, *Unknown Soldier*. | "Unknown is not the same as unimportant." History must not be written only by the winners. |
| **Aru Sembara** (Human, Battle Medic) | An exhausted but tireless healer who treats Divine and Infernal wounded side by side, and tolerates no fighting in her care. | War doesn't just kill people — it leaves the living without a reason to keep fighting. |
| **Kaede Veyr** (Human, Scout) | Finds safe routes, delivers supplies and messages between Silverlight camps, and collects the stories of everyone she meets along the way. | Still believes people can change. |

Kaede is the NPC most likely to accompany or guide the party once they leave Elysgard in Part II — she is the natural bridge between the sanctuary and the wider war.

## Running This Adventure

The central conflict of this adventure is **not good versus evil**. Both the Divine and Infernal races contain individuals driven by hatred, compassion, ambition, sacrifice, fear, and hope. Encourage the players to see the war through multiple perspectives before reaching the Archive, where the truth behind the conflict and the origin of Human Vaelithra are finally revealed.

Although the players witness many pivotal historical events, they are **not meant to alter history**. Instead, they become witnesses to the very moment that shaped the future of Dewanegara.

### DM Tips

**Let compassion win encounters.** Every combat in this adventure can be resolved, or at least de-escalated, through roleplay. The "Success" conditions given for each encounter are about protecting lives, not defeating enemies — reward players who look for a way to do both.

**No one hunting the Catalyst is truly evil.** The Radiant Inquisition, the Ashen Covenant, and Sōyō Gumi all genuinely believe capturing "the Catalyst" will save lives — they simply disagree, sometimes violently, about what "saving" means. Play them with sincerity, not malice.

**The Divine side of the war isn't united, and Part III should show that.** The Radiant Inquisition believes the Divine race is owed victory; Sōyō Gumi believes only that "the Sun shines upon all" and protects anyone under its light, Divine or Infernal. If the party has already met Kaelion, let Kiyomasa's Sōyō Gumi feel like a genuine relief by comparison — same side of the war, very different creed.

**Ser Kaelion Veyr and Mordren Ashveil are recurring rivals, not bosses.** Neither commander is meant to die in Part II. Both can retreat, protect their own wounded, and even end up helping the party if a bigger threat appears. The adventure isn't about defeating them — it's about the party surviving the war long enough to understand who the Catalyst really is. If you run this adventure again or extend it, consider letting Kaelion later witness Vaelithra save an Infernal soldier, or Mordren watch her save a Divine one — small moments that chip away at the prophecies both men believe in.

**The silver-eyed woman never kills.** She fights only to protect others, and never draws a weapon. Let her restraint say more about her than any dialogue could.

**Vaeil and Ra are gentle, not grand.** Neither god uses divine pressure or overwhelming power in this adventure. Play them as warm, patient, and — for the first time in their long lives — genuinely uncertain. They are old friends, not master and disciple: Ra can tell Vaeil "You think too much," and Vaeil isn't offended. Vaeil sees the future history could become; Ra sees the person standing in front of him right now — that difference is the heart of their friendship, and they don't always agree.

## Important NPCs

**Vaeil**, God of Time and Keeper of the Archive, founded Silverlight and refuses to take a side in the Sundering War. He isn't a distant, cryptic god — play him as warm, patient, quietly funny, and genuinely fond of simple conversation; he dislikes being worshipped and would rather ask a grieving soldier "Have you eaten?" than offer philosophy. He is, however, quietly exhausted — not physically, but from having seen too many ways this war could end, and none of them good. The players never physically see him; his voice resonates naturally throughout the Audience Chamber, gentle and timeless, without divine pressure. He answers every question honestly — including, for the first time in his existence, with "I do not know."

**Ra**, God of the Sun, is one of Vaeil's closest allies — and his opposite in temperament. Where Vaeil deliberates, Ra acts: bold, charismatic, confident, a little stubborn, protective of the people under his light, and quick to anger at injustice, but socially sharper than he lets on. He commands no army in the conventional sense — his followers, **Sōyō Gumi** ("the Sun's Host"), follow him not because he gives orders but because, when he arrives, *"the Sun has risen"* and they simply know what to do. Sōyō Gumi fights on the Divine side of the war but doesn't answer to it: their creed, *"the Sun shines upon all,"* puts them at odds with factions like the Radiant Inquisition, who believe the Divine race is owed victory. Long ago, Vaeil asked Ra to guard the road to the Infinite Archive and, should someone history itself cannot explain ever arrive, to bring them home — Ra agreed without knowing why, and never learned who he was watching for until she was standing in front of him. Ra is later entrusted with safeguarding the final Fragment of Vaeil's soul within the Solar Crest — not as a god receiving an order, but as one friend trusting another with a piece of himself.

**The Nameless Woman** is a mysterious silver-eyed woman drawn toward the Archive by a calling she cannot explain — not a voice or a vision, only a persistent, wordless longing for someone she has never met. She possesses no memories of her identity, fights only when someone else would otherwise die, and never uses a blade to kill. Having been born into the middle of the Sundering War, she is more survivor than sage: practical, fast, and efficient in a fight, having picked up her technique secondhand from the people she's saved along the way. She flinches from the word "Catalyst" — every place it follows her, someone gets hurt — and has no idea that she is quietly retracing Vaeil's own search for hope. She unknowingly becomes the first Human **Vaelithra**, the new Keeper of the Infinite Archive.

**Ser Kaelion Veyr** (Aasimar, Paladin) commands the **Radiant Inquisition**, a disciplined Divine Coalition task force convinced that the Catalyst will decide the outcome of the war and must not fall into Infernal hands. He is no butcher — he spares bystanders and refuses needless slaughter — but he genuinely believes "securing" the Catalyst, by force if necessary, is an act of mercy. His elite escort, the **Silverbinders**, carry chains built to suppress teleportation and planar escape.

**Mordren Ashveil** (Tiefling, Warlock) leads the **Ashen Covenant**, a warband of Infernal survivors, refugees-turned-soldiers, and demon-aligned veterans who believe the Divine Coalition will simply kill the Catalyst if they find her first. Mordren lost his home and his brother to the war; he doesn't want to slaughter the Divine race, only to stop losing to them — and he believes the Catalyst is the one chance to turn the war around, which makes him dangerous in a different way than Kaelion: he doesn't want to kill Vaelithra, he wants to use her.

## Preparing the Journal

Before the party departs Silverlight Sanctuary in Part I, an elderly archivist hands them a blank leather journal: *"History is written after it happens. Please... bring this one back filled."* Consider having a player keep an in-character log of the adventure in this journal — it can be a nice keepsake, and ties the party directly into Silverlight's oath to record the truth.
